# **App Name**: PrismShear

## Core Features:

- Immersive Hero Display: Engage visitors with a fullscreen video background, animated overlay text, and a prominent call to action for booking appointments.
- Service Showcase: Present detailed salon services within interactive cards that reveal pricing and specific details upon hover.
- Dynamic Portfolio Gallery: Display high-quality hairstyling work in an animated masonry grid, featuring image zoom and color shift effects on hover.
- Client Testimonials: Showcase glowing client reviews to build brand credibility and foster trust.
- Online Appointment Booking System: Facilitate seamless appointment scheduling with an interactive calendar UI, smooth form transitions, and loading animations for submission.
- AI Style Assistant Tool: Offer a generative AI tool that assists clients in articulating their desired hairstyle or suggests options based on their input, streamlining the consultation process.
- Responsive & Performance Optimized Design: Ensure a consistent, fast, and premium user experience across all devices and screen sizes with optimized loading and 60fps animations.

## Style Guidelines:

- A dark theme utilizing a deep desaturated blue-grey for the primary background (#1A2023), providing a sophisticated, high-contrast canvas that enhances neon elements.
- A vibrant electric blue (#2AADED) serves as the primary accent color for key elements, headlines, and calls to action, creating striking neon glow effects.
- A bright electric cyan (#00E1FF) acts as a secondary accent color, applied to interactive components and subtle highlights to further amplify the futuristic ambiance.
- Headlines will feature 'Space Grotesk', a bold sans-serif font, chosen for its distinctly tech-inspired and futuristic character. Large headings will also incorporate gradient text.
- Body text will utilize 'Inter', a clean, neutral, grotesque sans-serif font, ensuring excellent readability and a minimalist feel against the dark aesthetic.
- Custom, abstract line-art icons with integrated soft neon glow effects will be used, complementing the overall glassmorphism-inspired interface.
- An immersive fullscreen hero section with a cinematic video background and parallax scrolling will serve as the site's captivating focal entry point.
- A sticky, transparent navigation bar with a subtle blur effect will maintain site accessibility while visually blending with the dark, layered design.
- Content will be organized into logical, clearly defined sections (About, Services, Gallery, Testimonials, Booking) with a seamless flow enhanced by smooth transitions.
- The Gallery section will feature a dynamic masonry grid, allowing for an artful and responsive presentation of the salon's portfolio.
- Smooth scrolling with inertia, alongside elegant scroll-triggered animations (fade, slide, zoom), will create a fluid and engaging navigation experience.
- Interactive elements like buttons and cards will include subtle hover effects (e.g., glow, scale, 3D tilt) to provide intuitive visual feedback.
- A custom cursor effect, manifesting as a circle that expands upon hovering over interactive elements, will enrich the user's tactile engagement.
- Background elements will incorporate dynamic effects such as floating particles or light streaks to amplify the immersive, futuristic atmosphere.
- Animated gradient borders and restrained glitch or cyberpunk effects will be selectively used to reinforce the cutting-edge, tech-driven brand identity.
